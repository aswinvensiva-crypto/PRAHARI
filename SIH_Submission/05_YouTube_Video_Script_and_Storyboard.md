## PART A: 3-MINUTE SCRIPT (voice-over and dialogue)

### 0:00 to 0:15, OPENING HOOK (the problem, 15 seconds)
[Visual: drone or stock footage of a traffic jam on a hill-station highway, then a quick cut to an empty homestay courtyard in a nearby valley.]

NARRATOR: "Every long weekend, India's favourite hill stations choke with traffic that lasts for hours. Yet just a few kilometres away, beautiful homestays sit empty. India doesn't have too many tourists. They're in the wrong place at the wrong time."

### 0:15 to 0:45, SOLUTION OVERVIEW (30 seconds)
[Visual: PRAHARI logo animation, then a three-step loop graphic: Predict, Protect, Redistribute.]

NARRATOR: "Meet PRAHARI, an AI platform that predicts where tourists will go, measures each destination's live carrying capacity, and redirects surplus travellers to nearby 'twin' destinations and small hotels that have room.
It has five modules: Pulse forecasts demand. Balance rebalances crowds. Setu brings homestays online through WhatsApp and ONDC. Kavach keeps travellers safe. And Drishti gives officials a live command dashboard. It's all built on India's own digital public infrastructure: ONDC, Bhashini, UPI and NIDHI."

### 0:45 to 2:15, PROTOTYPE DEMO (90 seconds)

Demo 1: Traveller app (0:45 to 1:15)
[Screen recording: the PRAHARI web app on a phone mock-up.]

NARRATOR: "Priya wants to visit Manali this long weekend. PRAHARI Pulse shows a red forecast: twice the normal crowd, with long traffic delays expected. Instead of a dead end, PRAHARI Balance suggests Tirthan Valley. It has similar mountains and rivers, it's three hours closer, it's green, it has 38 verified homestays, and there's 15% off plus a free local experience. Priya books through ONDC and pays with UPI in under two minutes."

Demo 2: Host onboarding on WhatsApp (1:15 to 1:40)
[Screen recording: a WhatsApp chat in Hindi, photos being sent, a voice note, then the auto-generated listing card.]

NARRATOR: "Now meet Ramesh, a homestay owner in Tirthan. He simply says 'Namaste' on WhatsApp in Hindi, sends photos and a voice note, and PRAHARI creates a verified listing in under 10 minutes. Every week he gets a demand outlook and a fair price suggestion, and he pays a single-digit fee instead of a 15 to 25% commission."

Demo 3: Government dashboard (1:40 to 2:05)
[Screen recording: the Drishti dashboard map with hexagon heatmap, D-CCI status tiles and the what-if simulator slider.]

NARRATOR: "The Kullu District Tourism Officer sees Saturday's overload a week in advance on PRAHARI Drishti. With the what-if simulator, they test a permit cap and a twin-incentive campaign, see the overflow shift to the twins while revenue stays in the district, and alert police, transport and sanitation teams ahead of time."

Demo 4: Safety shield (2:05 to 2:15)
[Screen recording: a SACHET landslide alert pop-up, re-route suggestion, SOS button and fair taxi fare card.]

NARRATOR: "And if a landslide alert is issued, PRAHARI Kavach warns every tourist in the area within minutes, re-routes them, and connects them to 112 and the 1363 tourist helpline with one tap."

### 2:15 to 2:45, IMPACT AND NEXT STEPS (30 seconds)
[Visual: animated KPI cards appearing one by one.]

NARRATOR: "Our pilot targets are: 7-day forecasts within 15% error, 10 to 15% of peak overflow shifted to twin destinations, a 10 to 15 point rise in off-season occupancy for small stays, and distribution costs cut from up to 25% down to single digits.
Next, we plan a 6-month pilot in Kullu-Manali and the Nilgiris, then a rollout across 5 states, towards a national tourism Digital Public Infrastructure."

### 2:45 to 3:00, TEAM INTRO AND CALL TO ACTION (15 seconds)
[Visual: team photo, then each member's name and role on screen. No college name is shown.]

NARRATOR: "We are Team PRAHARI: machine learning, data engineering, backend, frontend, design and domain research, united by one goal.
PRAHARI: Predict. Protect. Redistribute. Let's make every corner of India a place worth visiting."

[End card: PRAHARI logo, tagline, "Smart India Hackathon | Student Innovation | Travel & Tourism".]
